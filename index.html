Questions we hear often

How long does a project take?

A company website usually takes 3–6 weeks. Web apps and mobile apps typically take 2–4 months, depending on features. You get a clear timeline before we start.

How much does it cost?

Every project is priced on its scope. After a free discovery call we send a written proposal with the price and a clear list of what is included.

Do you build for both iPhone and Android?

Yes. We usually build one cross-platform app that runs on both, which saves time and cost. When a project needs it, we build native apps for each platform.

Can you redesign my existing website or app?

Yes. We review what you have, keep what works and rebuild the rest without losing your content or search rankings.

What happens after launch?

We offer monthly support plans covering hosting, security updates, bug fixes and new features, so your product keeps improving.
